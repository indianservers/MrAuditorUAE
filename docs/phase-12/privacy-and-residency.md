# Privacy and residency acceptance

Evidence date: 18 October 2026. Scope: local synthetic release candidate.

Applicable regime must be established per tenant/controller, data subject and processing activity. References: [UAE Federal Decree-Law 45/2021](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download), [DIFC Law 5/2020](https://www.difc.com/business/laws-and-regulations/legal-database/difc-laws/data-protection-law-difc-law-no-5-2020), [ADGM Office of Data Protection](https://www.adgm.com/operating-in-adgm/office-of-data-protection). These references do not establish applicability or legal compliance for a customer.

Before customer data: record controller/processor roles, purpose/lawful basis, categories/subjects, storage/backup/log/support/AI regions, approved subprocessors and transfer basis; customer contract and retention/legal holds; access/export audit policy; incident contacts and response decisions. Tenants and objects are scoped; provider disclosure remains explicit. Technical export controls do not constitute a complete DSR system.

DSR runbook: verify requester identity/authority; establish applicable rights/exemptions and legal hold; identify all DB/object/model/provider references; minimize/redact other parties; obtain controller/professional approval; log request/decision/export or lawful deletion; verify backup/retention handling and response. Automated lawful deletion/DSR execution and configurable comprehensive retention are NOT_IMPLEMENTED. Do not delete immutable accounting/audit history automatically on subscription cancellation. Independent legal/security approval is outstanding.
