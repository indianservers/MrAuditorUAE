# Standards registry and coverage

Migration 0005 seeds 35 original metadata entries at rule version 1. No full copyrighted IFRS text or authoritative paragraph excerpts are embedded. Unverified issued dates remain null. Effective dates are populated for IFRS 18/19; other standards require technical validation of jurisdiction/period amendments. Coverage labels mean limited calculations, original checklist categories or reference metadata, never full implementation. See standards-coverage.json for every entry.

IFRS 18 applies to annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, with early application permitted. Earlier periods use IAS 1 unless the approved profile records early adoption and rationale. [IFRS Foundation IFRS 18](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/).

IFRS 19 has the same effective start date and permits early application. Election requires an eligible subsidiary without public accountability whose qualifying parent produces publicly available IFRS consolidated statements. Human-declared conditions are validated in the profile. Recognition and measurement continue under applicable IFRS standards. [IFRS Foundation IFRS 19](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-19-subsidiaries-without-public-accountability-disclosures/).

The original disclosure aid is not a licensed comprehensive checklist. IFRS 18 category totals and IFRS 19 election controls are foundations: MPM reconciliations, every consequential amendment and the complete reduced-disclosure catalogue remain unsupported. No blanket UAE legal/effective-date certification is made.
