# IFRS 15 limited-scope engine

Fixed price plus constrained variable consideration is allocated in relative standalone-selling-price proportions. The last obligation receives the allocation residual to preserve totals. Point-in-time recognition uses a supplied satisfaction date; over-time recognition uses approved progress and justification. Contract asset/liability are cumulative recognised revenue versus supplied billings.

The engine does not determine enforceability or satisfaction criteria. Modification analysis is documented but automatic modification classification/reallocation is unsupported. Significant financing, returns, warranties, principal/agent decisions and variable-consideration forecasting remain outside scope. A transaction300 at SSP100/200 allocates100/200; completed first obligation plus 50% second recognises200.

Reference: [IFRS Foundation IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/).
