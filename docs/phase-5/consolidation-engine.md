# Consolidation and translation foundation

Group assumptions identify unique engagement packages, one parent, ownership, all three control criteria, acquisition classification and sourced historical/average/closing rates. Member reports must be current, approved, accessible and share reporting dates. Majority ownership alone is insufficient.

Controlled entities are fully aggregated. Assets/liabilities use closing rates, equity historical rates and P&L/OCI average rates. Explicit translation OCI balances the translated data. NCI = (1−ownership) × translated subsidiary net assets. Scheduled goodwill/bargain purchase = consideration + acquisition NCI − acquisition net assets. Acquisition journals are not silently posted; investment/equity elimination must be explicitly supplied.

Balanced group-line eliminations require approved member source IDs. Unrealised profit debits expense and credits inventory using explicit accounts. Source entity reports remain unchanged. Intercompany Reconciliation exposes explicit elimination preparation/results; automatic counterparty matching is not implemented.

Common-control, asset-acquisition and equity-method classifications are recorded but calculations are rejected pending a validated policy. Step acquisitions, changes in control/ownership, complex NCI and hyperinflation are unsupported. Group cash flows/notes/acquisition posting still need independent completion, so all group runs remain DRAFT and cannot be released.

Independent fixture: parent assets130 at1 + subsidiary assets130 at2 =390. Average subsidiary rate1.5 gives group profit75. Ownership80% gives NCI52. Consideration100 + NCI20 − net assets100 gives scheduled goodwill20. Revenue/cost elimination10 preserves profit and the equation.
