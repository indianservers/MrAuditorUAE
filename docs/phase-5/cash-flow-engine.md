# IAS 7 cash-flow foundation

Indirect operating cash = profit + approved noncash adjustments + mapped working-capital cash impact. Working-capital impact is the negative debit-positive movement on explicitly classified asset/liability lines. Direct operating cash = approved receipts minus payments. Closing = opening mapped cash + operating + investing + financing + exchange effect. Closing must match source mapped cash exactly before release.

Interest/dividend/tax classification and schedule sources are human documented. Investing, financing, noncash and FX totals are approved inputs, not inferred from narratives. Cash equivalents are explicitly mapped. Acquisition cash, overdrafts, netting and complete IFRS 18 consequential classifications require technical review. Group cash-flow release is unsupported.

Reference: [IFRS Foundation IAS 7](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows.html/).
