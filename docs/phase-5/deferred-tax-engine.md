# IAS 12 and asset schedule foundations

Asset temporary difference = carrying amount − tax base; liability difference = tax base − carrying amount. Tax losses produce deductible differences. Difference × approved item rate gives a DTL-positive signed balance. DTA recognition and exceptions are human assessed; exceptions require rationale. Movement is new balance minus prior signed balance, allocated to P&L/OCI/equity/business combination. Rates require jurisdiction, source and effective date.

No statutory UAE tax return, Pillar Two, tax-base derivation or automatic recoverability assessment is implemented. Current tax is an approved input; no statutory rate is invented. Benchmarks include DTL4/DTA14 and OCI movement−4.

Fixed assets use daily actual/365 straight-line or stated diminishing rate, residual floors, components, disposals and documented revaluations. Impairment uses period-end forecasts, higher of VIU/FVLCD, goodwill-first loss and capped non-goodwill reversal. Provisions discount a human-selected estimate and reconcile explicit movements. These are limited IAS16/36/37 foundations.
