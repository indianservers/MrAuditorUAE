# Safe Harbours

Reviewed qualified-source transitional CbCR tests evaluate de minimis, simplified ETR and routine profits with UAE period rates, transition window and continuing eligibility. A separate three-year de minimis schedule is available. Unsupported elections block rather than silently lower tax. Full legal conditions require technical evidence; boolean attestations do not automate all guidance. Foreign QDMTT safe-harbour effects and non-material-entity simplified calculations are not implemented.
