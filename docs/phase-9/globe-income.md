# Globe Income

Current independently approved Phase 5 IFRS financial statements supply accounting profit. Entity, source engagement and period are checked. Signed reviewed adjustments cover net tax, dividends/equity, revaluation, disallowed costs, prior errors, pension, FX and intragroup items. Each needs evidence and a technical reference. Eligibility is professionally reviewed; it is not inferred from narration. Unrelated IFRS statements are never regenerated.
