# Top Up Tax

The deterministic engine calculates jurisdictional ETR, top-up percentage, excess profit after reviewed SBIE and additional top-up. Period-specific payroll/asset rates apply through 2032, then permanent rates. Allocations reconcile after cent rounding, assigning residual to a positive-income entity. No foreign IIR/UTPR or filing is included. Results remain draft technical workpapers, not approved returns.
