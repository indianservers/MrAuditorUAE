# Deferred Tax

Ordinary deferred amounts retain a lower recorded rate or cap a higher rate at 15 percent. Lower-rate assets are not universally uplifted. The limited GloBE-loss recast requires explicit loss attribution. Transition schedules require anti-avoidance/exclusion assessment. Due DTL recapture blocks the computation until a reviewed prior-year recalculation exists. Full Article 9.1 transfer rules, recapture history and loss-DTA utilization are not automated. A loss DTA candidate is not a booked asset.
