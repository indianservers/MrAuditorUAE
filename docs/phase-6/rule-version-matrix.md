# Phase 6 regulatory coverage matrix

Coverage describes the implemented scope, not legal certification. No source coverage means every provision in that instrument is supported.

- **Standard bands — Fully calculated and tested.** Sources: CT-RATES. Taxable income through AED375,000 at 0%; excess at 9%; approved version parameters.

- **Accounting-to-tax bridge — Fully calculated and tested.** Sources: CT-LAW. Approved IFRS PBT, source-bounded reviewed adjustments, approved exempt-income schedules.

- **Expense deductibility — Supported with manual professional input.** Sources: CT-LAW. Entertainment recipient scope/50%, fines, nonbusiness, explicit permanent/timing/other decisions; no blanket provisions add-back.

- **SBR — Supported with manual professional input.** Sources: MD73-2023 / MD131-2026. 3M current/history ceiling, election, residence, MNE/QFZP and anti-fragmentation facts; historical completeness reviewed.

- **QFZP conditions/activity — Supported with manual professional input.** Sources: CT-LAW / MD229-2025. Canonical evidence-backed conditions, reviewed contracts and transaction classifications; unresolved IP nexus blocked.

- **De minimis — Fully calculated and tested.** Sources: MD229-2025. Lower of5% applicable revenue and5M; explicit special-category denominator exclusions; no generic 9% nonqualifying-revenue shortcut.

- **2026 distribution procedures — Assessment checklist only.** Sources: FTA6-2026. Period/as-of gating, approved procedures rule, evidence-backed AUP condition; sample-size helper and 30-day submission due date; no auditor AUP report creation.

- **Interest limitation — Supported with manual professional input.** Sources: MD126-2023 / CTGIDL1. Statutory EBITDA bridge, pro-rated12M/30%, older carry first,10 subsequent periods, amortized capitalized interest; reviewed manual source balances.

- **Loss utilization — Fully calculated and tested.** Sources: CT-LAW37. 75% cap, eligible prior-period lots, immutable postings, serialized posting, correction reversals; no automatic eligibility conclusion.

- **Loss eligibility/transfer — Supported with manual professional input.** Sources: CT-LAW38/39. Continuity/same-business and75% common ownership facts reviewed; explicit prior-period transfer posted once; current-year loss transfer excluded.

- **Tax Group — Supported with manual professional input.** Sources: CT-LAW40–42 / CTP007. Full-period95% three-rights tests, aligned resident members, FTA and audit evidence; one group threshold, explicit consolidated tax adjustments.

- **Qualifying group/restructuring relief — Assessment checklist only.** Sources: CT-LAW26/27. Evidence-backed election/eligibility,75% qualifying group threshold and two-year clawback monitoring; no automatic tax-neutral journal.

- **Transitional — Supported with manual professional input.** Sources: CT-LAW61. First-period election, valuation evidence, manual assessed adjustment; standalone workpaper does not automatically post adjustment.

- **Domestic dividend/participation/foreignPE — Supported with manual professional input.** Sources: CT-LAW22–24 / MD302-2024. Reviewed canonical conditions and source-bounded exemption; complex eligibility/netting remains professional input.

- **Foreign tax credit — Supported with manual professional input.** Sources: CT-LAW47. Min foreign paid/UAE attributable, capped current tax; source-income ceiling and no overlap with exempt line; allocation is manual.

- **Related/connected persons — Assessment checklist only.** Sources: CT-LAW34–36. Dated relationships, contracts/accounts, risk review and separate approved adjustment link; no TP benchmarking.

- **Foundations — Assessment checklist only.** Sources: CTGFF1. Governance, beneficiaries, application and FTA approval evidence; never automatic transparency.

- **Returns/deadlines — Fully calculated and tested.** Sources: CT-LAW53. Nine-month calendar deadline plus explicitly approved overrides; checklist and current computation gate; external filing evidence only.

- **Registration/exemption2026 — Research/reference only.** Sources: FTA12-2026 / FTA15-2026. Manual event-based deadline assessments; no automatic application.

- **Pillar Two/special taxpayer computations — Not implemented.** Sources: N/A. Pillar Two blocks release; natural persons, transparent partnerships, exempt persons and foundations require specialist methodology.

Every profile selects approved rule IDs for its tax period and legal-as-of date. Computation snapshots include full selected rule bodies and source URLs. Later rule versions do not alter an existing run. Effective interval must contain the tax period, knowledge date cannot be after the selected legal-as-of date, and known dates for the three verified new decisions cannot predate issuance. New legal amendments are records and typed parameters; new legal formulas still require code and independent tests.
