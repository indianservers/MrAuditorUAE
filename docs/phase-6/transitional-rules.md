# Transitional rules and elections

The transitional workpaper requires an approved Article61 rule, asset category, acquisition date, first tax-period start, election evidence, accounting/tax values, reviewed methodology, valuation evidence and explicitly assessed adjustment. It preserves the professional election and supporting records. The calculator reproduces the assessed amount in an immutable specialist snapshot.

No automatic fair-value uplift, valuation, historical property allocation or disposal-gain formula is inferred. The assessed amount does not enter the corporate-tax bridge until prepared as a separate approved tax adjustment. Elections also have a generic effective-period register. Tests demonstrate an evidenced negative50k manual adjustment as a specialist result while the main tax computation remains unchanged.
