# Family foundation assessments

The workpaper captures founders/beneficiaries, governance evidence, holding structure, application, FTA approval evidence, statutory conditions, assessed treatment and ongoing compliance. Treatment can remain UNASSESSED or APPLICATION_PENDING. APPROVED_TRANSPARENT cannot be recorded without explicit FTA approval evidence.

This is an assessment checklist and document workflow, not an automatic determination of transparency or final foundation tax calculation. A foundation's reviewed profile classification is separate from the standard juridical-person computation. Tests demonstrate an APPLICATION_PENDING result and confirm that it does not silently remove corporate tax. Beneficiary-level computation, transparent-entity allocation and full MD261/2024 legal testing are not implemented.
