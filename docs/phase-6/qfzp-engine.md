# QFZP assessment and segmentation

The engine uses canonical evidence-backed conditions for substance, qualifying income/activities, excluded activities, transfer pricing, audited financials, election/status and compliance. Missing or contradictory facts return INSUFFICIENT_DATA/REVIEW_REQUIRED rather than a definitive qualification conclusion. Each segment identifies actual approved income line, contract evidence, counterparty type, jurisdiction, activity, revenue, taxable profit and reviewed classification. Revenue per line cannot exceed its financial source; total revenue reconciles to the profile.

De minimis threshold = lower of5% applicable revenue and AED5million. Explicit denominator exclusions require a special income category and legal rationale. The boundary is inclusive; excess fails. Incidental nonqualifying/excluded income follows the reviewed qualifying-income treatment only after de minimis passes. Separately assessed PE/property/IP categories receive their own tax treatment; a generic9% of nonqualifying revenue shortcut is not used. Qualifying-IP nexus calculations remain unsupported and prevent release. Prior disqualification is explicit; failure tracks five periods including the current period.

MD229/2025 applicability is retrospective to1June2023, with a separate knowledge date at/after issuance28August2025. Its activity classification remains professional contract review. For distribution periods commencing2026, after the verified FTA6/2026 knowledge date, an approved procedures rule and evidence-backed additional-procedures condition are required. The helper calculates conservative integer sample size ceil(N/(1+N×0.1²)); selection still requires licensed auditor procedures and highest-value documents. AUP submission due date is30days after the normal return deadline. No AUP audit report or ISRS4400 assurance is generated.
