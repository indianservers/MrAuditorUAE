# Tax AI assistance

Twenty-four task types cover profit bridge, ledger classification, deduction/entertainment, related/connected parties, interest, QFZP activity/contracts/exclusions/de-minimis, loss/group eligibility, relief/transitional summaries, technical memoranda, legislation changes, missing evidence, client-information drafts, reviewer exceptions, computation/prior-period differences, pending elections and management queries. These are assistance tasks; accepting a suggestion never posts an adjustment, approves a tax position or files a return.

Requests pass through the existing approved model/provider gateway. External client disclosure requires deployment approval configuration; diagnostic mock is the demonstrated provider. Context is bounded and contains selected scoped sources and approved rule metadata. Structured output requires issue, facts, legal references, exact tax period, analysis, potential treatment, missing facts, uncertainty, supporting references and required human action. requires_human_review must be true and tax_position_approved false. Rule ID/source/article/version and evidence citations must match the supplied allowlist; unsupported article numbers or periods fail closed.

Suggestions persist as SUGGESTED, UNDER_REVIEW, ACCEPTED or REJECTED, with model, execution, prompt version, rule snapshots and source versions. Independent reviewers cannot accept their own suggestions. Acceptance rechecks profile/rule/source versions. A separate human-prepared adjustment referencing an accepted suggestion still requires normal approval. Tenant hourly budget defaults to20 requests; the all 24-task integration test temporarily raises only its test setting and clears only its own test-budget key.

All 24 task transports and independent decisions are tested, with no direct posting; fabricated citations, source IDs and changed tax periods are rejected by unit tests. Diagnostic mock explicitly verifies transport/schema and does not provide semantic tax research. No live-provider semantic quality assessment, automatic web research, complete legislation retrieval or professional legal-opinion generation is claimed. Selected legal metadata is not a full-text statute corpus. Future provider rollout needs independent evaluations and approved data disclosure.
