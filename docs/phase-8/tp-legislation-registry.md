# Tp Legislation Registry

The registry in app/modules/tp/catalogue.py stores source identity, official URL, article, known-from date and legal applicability metadata. It never auto-approves a rule. Each engagement needs an independently reviewed rule adoption, exact period coverage and legal knowledge date. Updating metadata does not retrospectively change approved calculations.

Corporate Tax Articles 34–36 and 55 support arm’s length, relationship, connected-person and documentation reviews. MD97/2023 uses revenue of at least AED200m or MNE consolidated revenue of at least AED3.15bn for documentation. A reviewed UAE-headquartered non-MNE exception concerns Master File only. Counterparty exclusions still require individual professional assessment.

The FTA return guide uses strictly more than AED40m total RP transactions, then more than AED4m per category; dividends are excluded. CP disclosure uses more than AED500,000 per connected-person group. These are disclosure conditions, not exemptions from substantive arm’s length requirements. Downward tax adjustments need reviewed FTA approval.

APA guidance issued 30 December 2025 supports readiness only. AED100m is a materiality indicator, not unconditional acceptance. Cross-border commencement has not been independently verified. MD97 signing is not treated as its exact Gazette effective date.

Official references: [CT legislation register](https://tax.gov.ae/en/legislation/corporate.tax.aspx), [MD97/2023](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Ministerial-Decision-No.-97-of-2023-for-the-Purposes-of-Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022.pdf), [FTA return guide](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Guides/CT/CT-Returns-EN-11-11-2024.pdf), [FTA APA guide](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Guides/CT/APA-Guide-EN-30-12-2025.pdf). OECD guidance is methodology, not UAE legislation. Source metadata was checked on 18 October 2026; production adopters must review subsequent changes.
