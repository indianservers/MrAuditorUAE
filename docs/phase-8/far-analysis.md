# Far Analysis

Approved FAR workpapers record functions, assets, risks, actual conduct, risk-control capacity, tested-party considerations and interview gaps. Party identity and evidence references are scoped to the TP profile and engagement. Contracts alone do not establish actual conduct or risk control; the form requires those assessments explicitly.

FAR calculations produce a frozen reviewed-facts snapshot, not an automated characterization of a routine distributor or risk-bearing principal. Method selection references the approved FAR record. The Local File requires a typed approved FAR source in its FAR section. AI can propose questions and draft analysis, but source approval and independent acceptance remain separate.
