# Tp Methods

Method selection supports CUP, RPM, cost plus, TNMM, profit split and OTHER. A preparer identifies the tested party, controlled transaction population, PLI, economic characteristics and available comparables. Every unselected alternative must have a documented rejection rationale. No default TNMM or automatic best-method conclusion is applied.

A benchmark study must use the PLI in its approved method. Specialist observed-price studies require an explicit CUP/value methodology. The engine distinguishes gross cost-plus from net cost-plus and OP/OC from operating margin. Profit-split allocation and valuation are documented professional methods; a full multi-party residual profit-split calculator is not implemented.
