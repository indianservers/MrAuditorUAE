# Financial Segmentation

Segmentation starts from the current independently approved IFRS financial run. Every statement line must be allocated exactly once; missing or duplicate line codes fail. Statement section and chosen metric must agree. Source income signs are normalized explicitly and non-operating/other lines remain in the reconciliation trace.

Direct, financial and operational allocation drivers are documented in the typed workpaper. Decimal allocations round to eight places and assign the last-recipient residual so source totals reconcile exactly. Zero-total and duplicate-recipient drivers fail. Each segment computes gross profit, operating profit, operating margin, OP/OC, gross margin, gross cost-plus, net cost-plus and ROA. A zero denominator yields undefined, not zero profit performance.

Operating/non-operating classification and allocation economics remain professional judgments. The engine proves allocation conservation, not the commercial validity of a driver. The integration test reconciles the actual imported statement and obtains 60% operating margin on its test fixture.
