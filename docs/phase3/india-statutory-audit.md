# India statutory audit

Operational: engagement-bound Companies Act, CARO and disclosure checklists, using actual approved context, validated published rule references, applicability facts, procedures, management responses, conclusions and source evidence. States distinguish pending, applicable, not applicable, evidence required, exception identified and prepared. Independent review cannot approve unresolved clauses. File evidence requires a retained malware-cleared version; working papers must be current. Revisions preserve earlier conclusions and reviewers.

Routes: `/audit/india/statutory`, `/audit/india/caro`, `/audit/india/disclosures` and `/audit/india/financial-reporting`.

India statements reuse approved mappings and actual active trial balance snapshots, including comparative-only accounts. Division I requires AS; Divisions II/III require Ind AS. A reviewed disclosure/applicability case must explicitly select the division. Outputs retain mapping, snapshot and applicability source receipts, calculate credit presentation signs, profit and balance-sheet reconciliation.

Incomplete: a complete professionally validated Companies Act/CARO clause catalogue, automated statutory applicability exceptions, full Schedule III cash flow/equity/notes/presentation coverage and signed statutory opinions. Current statement output is a draft source-reconciled balance sheet and profit/loss working paper, not a complete statutory financial statement set. No clause wording or threshold was invented to fill missing official content.
