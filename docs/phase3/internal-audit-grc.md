# Internal audit and GRC

Reused the existing audit universe, controls, findings, remediation and monitoring modules. New internal-audit, management-letter and audit-committee report categories accept reviewed findings and working-paper references. Country evidence workflows include controls/vendor and other operational checks over explicit source tables.

Findings and remediation remain assigned, permission-controlled audit records. AI catalogue additions provide draft commentary, variance and disclosure blocker assistance through the existing grounded gateway; AI does not approve evidence, opinions, tax positions or remediation closure. Production monitoring, agency autonomy and complete sector-specific control libraries require further qualification.
