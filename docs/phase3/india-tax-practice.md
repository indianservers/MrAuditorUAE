# India tax practice

Operational: versioned rule assessment, tax-audit evidence checklists, source-backed arithmetic and independent review. Numeric inputs must agree to reviewed sources; missing legal rules or provenance block conclusions. Accounting document comparisons, statutory dues, payroll and withholding evidence workflows are available through the connected country-workflow engine. They reconcile supplied source tables; they do not certify tax liability, input credit or filing completeness.

Incomplete: a comprehensive period-qualified Income Tax Act 1961/2025 transition package, all applicable tax rates/elections, full tax audit statutory clause content, GST/TDS/TCS government portal adapters and filing exports. Do not apply legacy 3CA/3CB/3CD material to every 2026 period. The official legacy help page was retrieved; the transition FAQ could not be independently retrieved. A source hash or portal help page does not resolve which legal regime applies.
