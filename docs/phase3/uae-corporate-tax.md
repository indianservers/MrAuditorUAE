# UAE corporate tax

Reused existing CT, QFZP and VAT engines, reviews and calculation history. The Phase 3 report adapter accepts a tax run only through an approved release record and current engine source dependencies. Access uses the existing `tax.profile.read` permission, including the lineage registry. This fixes a mismatched permission name that could hide tax lineage.

New QFZP and DMTT applicability evidence checklists retain published effective-dated rules and actual procedures, facts, exceptions and evidence. These do not replace a complete legal determination. Existing UAE regulatory catalogues are preserved; this phase does not assert that their presence independently validates every 2026 election or tax outcome. No new unverified threshold was published.

Production needs period-specific professional review, full legal/source qualification and filing controls. Outputs remain draft and human-approved conclusions remain mandatory.
