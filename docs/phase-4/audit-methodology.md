# Methodology and professional standards

Verified against official IAASB sources on 18 October 2026. The application requires a profile to record the target period, standards edition, applicability rationale and independent professional validation. A handbook's publication date does not make every included standard effective for that reporting period.

The [2026 IAASB Handbook](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/2026-handbook-international-quality-management-auditing-review-sustainability-and-other-assurance) was issued in September 2026 and includes standards that become effective later in December 2026. For a calendar-year period beginning 1 January 2026, the new fraud/going-concern revisions are not automatically the mandatory effective editions merely because the 2026 handbook contains them.

[ISA 240 (Revised)](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/isa-240-revised-auditor-s-responsibilities-relating-fraud-audit-financial-statements) and ISA 570 (Revised 2024) apply to periods beginning on or after 15 December 2026, subject to adoption and any permitted early application. The application does not silently apply an exposure draft or early adoption. The [IAASB fraud and going-concern resources](https://www.iaasb.org/focus-areas/fraud-going-concern-revised-standards-enhance-public-trust) distinguish the issued revisions.

[ISA 315 (Revised 2019)](https://www.iaasb.org/news-events/2019-12/iaasb-enhances-and-modernizes-isa-315-more-robust-risk-assessment) applies to periods beginning on or after 15 December 2021. [ISA 220 (Revised)](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/international-standard-auditing-220-revised-quality-management-audit-financial-statements) applies to periods beginning on or after 15 December 2022. [ISQM implementation resources](https://www.iaasb.org/focus-areas/quality-management) cover the quality-management standards effective from December 2022; ISQM 1 concerns the firm's system, while ISQM 2 concerns applicable engagement quality reviews. [ISA 600 (Revised)](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/quality-management-and-group-audits-highlighting-certain-aspects-interaction-between-isa-220-revised) applies to group-audit periods beginning on or after 15 December 2023.

Traceability by purpose: ISA 200/210 inform acceptance and responsibilities; ISA 220/ISQM 1/2 inform review policies; ISA 230 informs preserved documentation; ISA 240/315/330 inform risk and responses; ISA 300 informs planning; ISA 320 informs documented materiality; ISA 450 informs findings/misstatements; ISA 500/520/530 inform evidence, analytics and sampling; ISA 540/550 address estimates and related parties; ISA 560/570/580 inform completion considerations; ISA 600 addresses group-specific scope; ISA 700 and related reporting standards identify future reporting boundaries. This is a functional mapping, not a claim that a generic checklist implements every requirement.

Methodology versions are immutable. A separate authorized human must approve a prepared profile. Instantiation checks tenant, approval and exact reporting-period applicability, then creates editable engagement procedures with the methodology version reference. The original starter includes all 20 requested account areas and configurable working-paper sections, independence questions and benchmark options. It is a drafting aid requiring tailoring, not a complete commercial audit manual or proprietary program copy.

Independent technical validation remains required for UAE adoption, regulator/free-zone requirements, entity-specific applicability, early adoption, ethics/independence requirements, audit methodology completeness and any subsequent amendments. The application does not independently certify local legal compliance or reproduce copyrighted standards text. Group audit execution and final financial statement/opinion generation are not implemented in Phase 4.
