Historical India nonresident withholding observations

The india-27q-regular-v7.5 profile adds original Form 27Q regular statement
observations to the existing India withholding workflow. It reuses retained
evidence, clean scanning, queued source preparation, independent review,
reported-only liability comparison, party/challan lineage, complete XLSX and
source replacement staleness. UAE and other-country domestic engines cannot
activate this India profile. Historical source periods remain explicitly
declared rather than inferred from the engagement or upload date.

The scope is FY 2010-11 through 2025-26. Successor Form 144, correction
statements and salary forms remain separate. Protean's regular-download page
was rechecked on 30 October 2026. The original version 7.5 XLS, original public
sample ZIP and its unchanged extracted TXT are retained under
authority-references/protean-legacy-2025-05-27/. Exact source hashes and URLs
are recorded in 27Q-source-assessment.json. The date in the specification URL
is not inferred to be a legal commencement date.

The numbered tables and original sample both establish 18 FH, 72 BH, 41 CD and
54 DD fields. The general-notes example still says 16 FH fields; that example
does not override the actual numbered layout. The combined lexical limits
retain the exact 27Q specification hash. No existing 26Q/27EQ field limits or
output columns are changed. A further source inconsistency remains: the
15CA-reference field's numeric width is 12, while its description mentions
12 through 15 characters. This conservative observed-file profile applies
the numbered width of 12, so longer references need a separately assessed
layout; it does not silently truncate them or claim complete FVU compatibility.

Separate original crosswalk evidence and explicit independent population,
component, allocated-deposit, gross-challan and payment-base controls are
required. The public sample contains two distinct parties, 166,000 INR tax,
26,600,000 INR payment base and 20,000,000 INR gross challan deposit. These
different amounts remain separate. Original challan/BSR identifiers retain
their leading zeros. Purchase-value controls belong to TCS and are rejected
for this TDS source. Nil and unknown book-entry flags cannot bypass controls.

Party review and export retain the uploaded rate-basis code, remittance code,
15CA reference, residence-country code, contact/address, foreign tax ID,
recorded cash-withdrawal bands and 115BAC opt-out flag. Blank optional fields
stay blank. A and B are source rate-basis claims, not assessed entitlement to
the Income Tax Act or a treaty rate. The importer requires explicit supported
rate-basis and numeric remittance/residence codes from FY 2013-14, and an
explicit recorded Y/N opt-out flag from FY 2023-24. These bounded checks do
not establish the complete issuer conditional-field or code-membership rules.

A crosswalk group cannot merge different recorded rate bases, remittance
types, residence countries or foreign tax identifiers. Missing-PAN sentinels
do not establish one person's identity: grouping those parties additionally
requires the same retained name and a nonempty identical foreign tax ID.
Separate original parties remain traceable even where amounts are grouped.
These controls do not authenticate a foreign tax identifier or residency.

The prepared statement can supply reported observations only. Treaty
eligibility, TRC/Form 10F, beneficial ownership, PE exposure, section/rate
qualification, Form 15CA/15CB authenticity, government acceptance, CSI, FVU,
bank execution and posted tax credit require separate evidence/procedures.
No tax rate is calculated from the uploaded recorded-rate field. Historical
superseded/cancelled records preserve their amounts and selected status.
