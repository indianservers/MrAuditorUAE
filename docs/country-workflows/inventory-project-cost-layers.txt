Segregated purchased project-lot cost attribution

The shared IFRS inventory record now accepts identification_basis
segregated_project_lot under method specific_identification. UAE/AED and
India/INR use the existing entity reporting profile, period, approval and
immutable calculation lifecycle. This is financial inventory attribution;
country tax valuation and framework eligibility are not inferred.

Each item/location represents one explicitly assessed, non-interchangeable
segregated purchased project lot. Keep unrelated units and projects separate.
Provide SKU, identification_reference and identification_assessment. Identity,
ownership, UOM and identification basis must remain consistent across locations.

Opening stock uses opening_layers with distinct reference, positive quantity,
nonnegative unit_cost and explicit evidence. Their quantities must exactly equal
opening_quantity. Set opening_unit_cost to zero and leave opening_cost_evidence
empty: a pooled opening average is rejected, and layer costs determine value.
Opening layer references cannot collide with receipt or issue references.
Every receipt creates a new identified layer at actual quantity times unit cost
plus declared landed cost, supported by its retained evidence reference.

Every issue supplies cost_allocations: layer_reference, quantity and evidence.
Allocation quantities must exactly cover the issue. Duplicate targets, missing
or future layers, whitespace-only evidence and cumulative layer overdrafts are
rejected. Partial issues draw only from the selected layer's actual residual
cost. Full exhaustion consumes its exact remaining cost. No FIFO fallback,
cross-layer average, undisclosed allocation or balancing amount is invented.
Layer quantities and costs must reconcile to the movement ledger at its declared
eight-decimal accounting precision. Per-layer and allocation outputs preserve
their full retained Decimal precision, including division residuals.

Transfers retain the declared project identity, basis, SKU, UOM, ownership,
quantity, temporal order and actual allocated issue cost. A mismatched transfer
remains a blocking reconciliation issue. The receiving layer's evidence is
separate from the outgoing allocation evidence. This does not independently
verify the physical transfer or original-file contents.

The 20,000-row limit counts items, movements, opening layers and allocations.
The result retains all layers, including exhausted zero balances, and every
issue allocation. Separate paged tables show source and allocation evidence
without rounding numeric strings or silently truncating later records. Existing
per-item NRV, write-down and custody-only exclusions remain. The appropriateness
of grouping goods for a single project-lot NRV requires professional assessment.

Approved input changes stale previous runs; previous values remain immutable.
The accounting implementation fingerprint includes inventory_layers.py, so an
operative layer-engine change cannot reuse a previous engine attestation.

All examples and automated API cases are synthetic. No client pilot approval,
production/overhead allocation, returns lifecycle, manufacturing WIP accounting,
physical count, legal title, automatic tax treatment or statutory opinion is
established by this extension. India legal-framework adoption still requires
independent assessment; the linked MCA PDF returned HTTP 403 during this check.
