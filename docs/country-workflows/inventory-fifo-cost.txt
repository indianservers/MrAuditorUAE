Assessed FIFO cost roll-forward — shared UAE and India workflow

Workflow: inventory-fifo-cost. Contract: inventory-fifo-cost/1.
Sources: movements (inventory-cost-movements), books (stock).
Both sources require independent review. Closing books require independent
directional quantity controls. The explicit cost window must lie inside the
engagement period; opening layers must be dated at that window's start.

Each movement retains an exact identifier, date, sequence, kind, positive
quantity, site, SKU, lot, unit, currency, original support version/hash,
reference and assessment. Opening/receipt unit cost is assessed and nonnegative;
an issue's cost is calculated from oldest then-available layers. Date/sequence
ties are rejected. Sequence explicitly declares opening-layer order.

Independent controls declare row count, opening/receipt/issue quantities,
opening/receipt extended costs and rounding method/places for every exact
partition. Zero is supplied explicitly. No cross-partition offset, FX conversion,
unit conversion, aggregate-value netting or inferred costing policy is applied.
The supported population is 1–5000 events, with no omitted allocation rows.

Results retain issue-to-layer allocations, movement covered costs, unresolved
issue quantities, closing layers, independent controls, raw and rounded closing
costs, rounding differences, covered issue cost and raw cost conservation.
Book gross value is quantity multiplied by book unit cost, compared using the
same declared rounding. Nonzero rounded differences are exceptions. An issue
without then-available stock blocks release; later receipts cannot backfill it.
Remaining layers after such a shortage are explicitly provisional, with no
complete FIFO closing valuation asserted.

Original cost/policy support must be distinct from the normalized movement CSV.
The retained-document verifier permits at most 50 distinct supporting originals
and 100 MiB in total; larger populations require separately scoped reviewed runs.
Source version/hash checks use actual retained bytes. Replacing support stales
the source/result without rewriting its historical body. Source contributors
cannot approve their own derived result. Independent approval also requires the
current FIFO contract and retained-document verification marker.

The UI provides controlled source entry, explicit policy/window assessment and
independent 20-row pages for allocations, events, closing layers and controls.
The XLSX retains complete dedicated sheets, independent of page projections.

Scope: assessed gross FIFO arithmetic, reusable in UAE/AED and India/INR and
other explicitly declared currencies. This does not select an accounting or tax
framework, establish physical existence/title/NRV, calculate manufacturing or
landed-cost allocation, infer returns/transfers/revaluations, support weighted
average/specific identification, cost negative stock, or issue an audit opinion.
Those require their own supported workflows and independently assessed evidence.

Primary context consulted 2026-10-30:
https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-2-inventories/
The IAS 2 overview describes cost and lower-of-cost/NRV measurement and FIFO or
weighted-average formulas for interchangeable inventory. It is context for this
limited arithmetic workflow, not evidence that a given entity must adopt FIFO.
No native statutory filing or country legal qualification is claimed.

Validation inputs are authored synthetic fixtures, including both country
contexts and exact 5000-event population. They are separate from the empty
real-client pilot intake folders. Passing them does not qualify real clients.
