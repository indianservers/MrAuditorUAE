Specific identification: individual purchased units

Shared financial-accounting costing is available through the existing approved
IFRS inventory input and calculation workflow for UAE and India. Select method
specific_identification. Each item/location must represent one separately
identified, non-interchangeable physical unit. Supply SKU, identification_reference,
identification_assessment and opening_cost_evidence where opening quantity is one.
Opening quantity is zero or one; each receipt or issue is exactly one unit.
Zero opening quantity requires zero opening unit cost. Distinct unit costs are
never averaged together. Per-unit NRV and custody exclusions remain in force.

The combined population is bounded to 20,000 item/movement rows. Across locations,
an identity requires exactly one original acquisition, consistent ownership/UOM
and a non-overlapping dated custody sequence. Existing explicit same-day sequence
rules apply. Transfers preserve identity, quantity and actual cost; unmatched
transfers produce blocking reconciliation issues without fabricated adjustments.
Schedule and movement rows retain identity and the approved cost evidence.

This does not establish method eligibility, physical existence, legal title,
independent source-document verification or India tax/ICDS valuation. Evidence
references are approved input narratives, not automatic proof of original files.
Professional assessment remains necessary. Pooled project lots, production cost
allocations and returns remain separate unimplemented costing extensions.

Accounting basis: IAS 2 paragraphs 23–24 and Ind AS 2 paragraphs 23–24.
https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-2-inventories/
https://www.mca.gov.in/Ministry/pdf/IndAS2_2020_10112020.pdf

All added arithmetic and custody fixtures are synthetic; no client qualification
or statutory assurance is inferred from passing them.
