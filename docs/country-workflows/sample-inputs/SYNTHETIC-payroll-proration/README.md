# Synthetic dated PF wage examples

These are authored mechanical examples, not client evidence or qualified legal
rules. All wages, membership choices, ceiling values, dates and rates are supplied
assumptions. The three cohorts distinguish newly assessed eligible periods,
assessed actual EPF wage bases with later EPS eligibility, and assessed capped
bases changing during a month.

Prepare the three source files using their exact SHA-256, row counts and currency
controls in `controls.json`. For `rules.csv`, select a separately retained clean
synthetic policy document. Source preparation binds its UUID and hash; no fake
document identity is included in the CSV. Independently review all three sources
before selecting India dated PF wage-basis review.

The uncapped segment control is INR 180,000.00. Monthly post-ceiling component
bases total INR 129,333.31 across nine rows. Each total counts component bases
separately and is not gross payroll or contributions. The derived source needs
its own independently entered count and amount and a separate source review.
Apply whole-month contribution rate profiles without a second ceiling or wage
slab. Due dates, remittance returns and bank evidence remain separate inputs.

`authored_expected` is a synthetic regression aid. Do not copy it into the
independent client pilot folders or describe it as independently approved.
