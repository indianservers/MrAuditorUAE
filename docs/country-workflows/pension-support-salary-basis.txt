UAE pension: salary consistency before government-support netting

The employer-minus-government calculation previously resolved support for the
same employee, registration, month and pension scope without comparing the two
declared contribution-account salaries. Contradictory salaries could therefore
produce a matched net employer amount, including an incorrectly resolved zero
support amount after crossing an eligibility threshold.

Salary-dependent support now requires exact numeric equality of the employer
and government assessed salaries before applying caps. Support is salary-dependent
when its formula uses a rate, has a positive minimum basis, or has an exclusive
salary eligibility ceiling. Equal capped values do not resolve different raw
salaries. Equivalent decimal strings do not create a false mismatch.

GOVERNMENT_SUPPORT_SALARY_BASIS_MISMATCH leaves the expected net employer amount
and difference unresolved, marks the total partial and blocks independent result
approval. It retains the government source ID, assessed base, salary reference
and whether a matching salary was required. The compact results table shows the
government base beside the employer base. Full XLSX retains the conflict and
its source details. No salary or support amount is silently substituted.

An explicitly fixed support amount with no salary-based minimum, cap or
eligibility ceiling remains independently assessed; an irrelevant zero salary
does not change that fixed amount. Independent membership, eligibility and
salary-definition assessments remain required. No authority, cohort, statutory
rate, threshold, entitlement or rounding method is newly supplied by this fix.

The new arithmetic examples use deliberately fictional contribution rates.
One regression first reproduced the false unblocked result. Final unit cases
cover threshold-driven zero support, different raw salaries hidden by a cap,
equivalent decimal representations and genuinely salary-independent fixed
support. Connected published-profile APIs verify the otherwise-equal aggregate
reported total remains blocked, approval returns 409, the mismatch is exported,
and prior source-withdrawal staleness still works. Local scanner/dispatch
boundaries remain explicit doubles.

Browser assertions added to the existing UAE release case use real hosted
scanner/worker/source reviews, then check both visible salaries and disabled
approval. Their execution and original screenshot inspection remain pending.
No real client or statutory qualification is claimed by these synthetic checks.
