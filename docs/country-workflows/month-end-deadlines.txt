Explicit preservation of a triggering month-end

The shared DeadlineRule now supports month_end_policy=preserve_month_end.
When the trigger is the last day of its month, a month offset ends on the last
day of the target month. Other trigger dates keep their corresponding day,
capped by the target month's length. Any separate day offset is applied after
the month calculation. Later-of candidates use the same explicitly adopted
policy before selecting the latest date and applying the rolling convention.

The default remains clamp and is omitted from stored serialization, preserving
existing corresponding-day behavior and historical bodies. Choosing UAE or India
does not select a policy. The fixed adopted DMCC profile rejects a changed policy
until separately adopted; this increment cannot silently extend its timetable.
Business-day counting rejects an unrelated month policy instead of ignoring it.

Review of the official current Companies Regulations PDF exposed the gap:
section 417 distinguishes corresponding-day and month-end calculations. The
50-page guidance's general corresponding-date summary was insufficient to
establish all boundary dates. The unchanged consolidated 633-page PDF and
complete rendered pages 252-253 are retained with exact hashes in the separate
month-end-source-assessment.json. Those relevant sections were read and the
two pages visually inspected. The entire regulation is not claimed qualified.
The filename version date is not an inferred commencement date for every rule.

For example, a declared June 30 trigger plus nine months produces March 31
under preserve_month_end, compared with March 30 under the existing default.
This difference is shown in the server-derived comparison breakdown. Changing
the declared policy without a matching saved deadline yields reassessment.
Filing readiness identifies the explicitly declared policy, alongside the rule
and fact evidence, without asserting statutory applicability or an exemption.

Both countries' real PostgreSQL/HTTP cases cover explicit month-end policy and
the default, independent source and filing approval, false period-end and
declared-deadline rejection, complete candidate dates and unchanged stale
historical bodies after fact withdrawal. Unit cases include leap-year triggers,
ordinary dates, separate day offsets, tied/winning candidates, legacy omission,
unknown-policy rejection and DMCC/business-day guards. API scan and dispatch
boundaries remain explicit doubles. No portal submission or extension is inferred.
