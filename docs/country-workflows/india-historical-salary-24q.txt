Historical salary TDS observations: Form 24Q Q1-Q3 v6.3

india-24q-regular-q1q3-v6.3 reuses the existing historical withholding reader
and independent controls, including complete FH/BH/CD/DD populations, tax,
surcharge, cess, allocated deposit, payment base and gross challan totals.
Its salary header is SL1. The historical fiscal-year controls remain bounded
to FY2010-11 through FY2025-26. Successor Form138 remains a separate profile.

Salary records retain original employee references, PAN, section code, payment,
deduction and recorded deposit dates, reason and certificate references.
24Q has no reported tax-rate field. No rate is invented, copied from another
form or inferred from employee tax/payment amounts. Employee references retain
leading zeros. Salary source payment and deduction dates must fit the declared
quarter. Crosswalk groups cannot merge employee references or certificate
contexts; missing-PAN employee observations require separate groups.

The exact issuer workbook, ZIP and unchanged quarterly sample are retained in
authority-references/protean-legacy-2025-05-27/. The source assessment records
original hashes, source URLs and record widths. The sample's recorded deposit
precedes its deduction date. This observation is preserved without inferring
payment legality, tax credit or entitlement. The corresponding unsupported
chronology rejection in the dated Form138 observation reader has been removed.
Positive observed amounts still need their original dates, and dates beyond the
explicit observation cutoff remain rejected. No bank evidence is manufactured.

Original files and separately retained crosswalk evidence use the existing
protected upload, scanner, queued preparation, separate reviewer, reported-only
withholding comparison and complete XLSX export. Exports and UI retain component
tax and employee reference/date fields without fabricated rate or deposited flag.
Replacing the crosswalk invalidates the reviewed source and downstream result
without rewriting the historical body or version.

Q4 is explicitly excluded from this profile. Its retained numbered workbook
v7.5 contains SD, S16, C6A, 94P, P16 and P6A annual detail populations. The public
Q4 sample has BH70/DD48/SD80 rather than the numbered BH72/DD54/SD88 layout.
The sample is not silently widened or used as a valid v7.5 oracle. Only the exact
text member was read from its original ZIP; unrelated members were not extracted.

sample-inputs/SYNTHETIC-historical-24q/ contains fictional independent controls
for exercising the unchanged public quarterly sample. These are not a client
pilot or professional approval. The existing 154-case authored engine catalogue
and real pilot count remain unchanged.

Broader salary qualification remains open: Q4 annual populations and calculations,
source/sample version reconciliation, conditional legal/certificate eligibility,
complete payroll/cohort rules, correction and CSI/FVU handling, current portal
filing/payment/credit evidence, and independently approved real client pilots.
