India company reporting scope workpaper

Open an India engagement for the exact financial period, then use Assess India
company audit, CARO and IFC reporting scope in Country workflows. The API is
POST /api/v1/intelligence/engagements/{eid}/india-company-reporting-scope.

The workpaper keeps small-company classification, Companies Act section 139
audit, CARO standalone matters, consolidated paragraph 3(xxi), and section
143(3)(i) IFC reporting separate. CARO/IFC exemptions do not remove the separate
company-audit candidate. Unknown flags and blank INR amounts remain unknown.
Current-year revenue, immediately preceding-year turnover, latest audited
turnover and the two borrowing populations are separate inputs.

Retain eight distinct latest clean evidence versions: Companies Act, small-
company amendment, CARO original order, CARO commencement amendment, IFC
notification, IFC corrigendum, separate period-specific legal adoption, and
separate company facts. Their evidence version IDs and SHA256 values are saved.
Original bytes are verified in the private object store. Three Gazette files
are pinned to the retained source-assessment.json. The Act, CARO order and IFC
corrigendum identities are independently assessed; no automatic authenticity
or complete legal-chain qualification is claimed. The ICAI guidance reproduction
does not replace these original legal documents.

The adoption window must cover the exact engagement period and assessment
date. Unresolved adoption, other operative changes or any unresolved candidate
blocks independent approval, both in the UI and API. Uploaders and the workpaper
preparer cannot independently approve their own contribution. Replacing any
source version makes old results stale without rewriting the retained result.
Existing complete row XLSX/JSON exports and event review apply.

The core covers CARO 2020 periods commencing from 1 April 2021. It uses the
small-company definition effective 1 December 2025 only for eligible balance
sheet dates; earlier definitions remain pending. Financial IFC thresholds are
strict and joined with AND pursuant to the 2017 corrigendum. Filing-default
facts affect private-company IFC exemption separately. Special regimes and
foreign-company audit/IFC scope remain pending where not established.

Workflow review is not a statutory opinion, audit completion or authenticated
legal adoption. AS/Ind AS selection, Schedule III division and complete reporting
clauses remain separate work. Synthetic tests, including the browser case that
deliberately retains unqualified guidance copies and leaves adoption unresolved,
are not real client qualification. Real UAE/India pilots belong under pilot-data.
