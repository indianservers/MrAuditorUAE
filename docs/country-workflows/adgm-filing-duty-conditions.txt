Restricted scope: Chapter 8 applicability considers both Registrar notice and the independently assessed non-registrable publication trigger under sections 411(7), 412 and 415(3). A missing notice is not an assessed negative. Publication is not inferred merely because a company has financial statements or a website.

Unlimited companies: the section 422 assessment retains the three limited-undertaking control/parent conditions over the reference period, the financial-institution/group exclusion, and the all-members limited/qualifying-unlimited exclusion. Every applicable exclusion needs an explicit assessment. Ownership, knowledge and cross-jurisdiction entity classification are assessed evidence, not automatically authenticated registry facts.

Dormant subsidiaries: sections 423-425 remain separate from an audit exemption or an exemption from preparing accounts. Checks cover subsidiary and whole-financial-year dormancy, PIE/financial-institution exclusions, all-member agreement, the authenticated parent guarantee with its identity/date/year/liability scope, inclusion in the parent's IFRS consolidated accounts, and the notes disclosure. A failed condition prevents that exemption; an unknown condition does not become false or satisfied.

The individual-accounts and dormant-subsidiary-supporting-documents procedures are separate obligations. Even where individual accounts are assessed exempt, five supporting filings remain: members' agreement notice, authenticated parent guarantee statement, parent consolidated accounts, auditor's report on those accounts, and parent consolidated annual report. All five deliveries and their timely filing need explicit retained assessment before the individual-accounts exemption is established. The supporting-document obligation can be scheduled using the existing exact timetable before actual delivery. Its review does not establish that individual accounts are already exempt or that the documents have been filed. Timeliness remains an independently assessed input, not an automatically authenticated portal result.
