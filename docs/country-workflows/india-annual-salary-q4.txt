The retained source filename starts with FY2011-12; the adopted observation
profile is explicitly bounded to FY2011-12 through FY2025-26 and Q4. Earlier
Q4 layouts and successor Form138 remain separate. The v7.5 public Q4 sample
has BH70/DD48/SD80, unlike the numbered BH72/DD54/SD88. Its original bytes remain
unchanged, and the reader rejects it rather than widening it silently.

The shared historical reader continues to control the quarterly FH/BH/CD/DD
population, tax components, challan totals and independently assessed crosswalk.
The Q4 profile additionally retains every original SD/S16/C6A/94P/P16/P6A record,
including its source line, parent identity, original PAN and all numbered fields.
These annual records are not appended to or double-counted as quarterly TDS rows.

Required independent annual controls declare all six record counts, including
explicit zeros; salary gross total income, taxable income, net tax payable,
annual tax deducted and signed shortfall/excess; and specified-senior gross
income, taxable income and recorded net tax payable. The assessment accompanies
the retained independent crosswalk. Source preparation, original hashes, separate
reviewer, allowed reported-only role and source replacement freshness use the
existing protected workflow. No source can serve as its own crosswalk.

The reader checks exact widths and full source sequence, annual batch/parent
binding, child serials and distinct recorded section IDs, child counts and sums,
batch income/count controls and selected recorded-component arithmetic. C6A's
deductible amount is field7; P6A's is field9. Signed salary other-head income and
shortfall are retained. Period-specific source fields and recorded regime flags
govern the adopted component checks. A recorded flag is not verified legal
eligibility. Original landlord/lender identities, employment and superannuation
details remain source observations requiring independent assessment.

The UI pages every annual record and every numbered field, with separate type
filtering and field pagination. Blank values remain distinct from explicit zero
or a negative amount. XLSX exports one complete sheet per annual record type
plus independent control comparisons; original field strings remain unchanged.
Review history remains unchanged and becomes stale when the crosswalk changes.

Source limitations remain explicit. SD field80 has conflicting earlier-year
wording; this profile adopts its explicit FY2023+ applicability sentence. The
194P recorded net-after-relief value is controlled independently; no formula is
asserted where the numbered workbook supplies none. This is selected recorded
arithmetic and population validation, not complete conditional FVU/CSI, legal
deduction/certificate entitlement, tax recomputation or all-quarter reconciliation.
Rates, tax due, current filing, actual payment and credit are not established.
