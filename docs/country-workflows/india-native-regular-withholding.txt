Controlled historical India regular withholding observations

Profiles: india-26q-regular-v7.8 and india-27eq-regular-v6.9. These reuse
original evidence storage, scanning, queued source jobs, source/result review,
withholding liability comparison, lineage, complete export and staleness.
They are available only for India engagements. They accept the selected ASCII
CRLF FH/BH/CD/DD layouts for FY 2010-11 through 2025-26. Forms 140/143 from
TY 2026-27, salary/nonresident forms and correction statements are separate.

This is an observed-amount import, not government file validation. Exact field
counts and pinned issuer field-width limits are checked, together with record
sequence/parentage, TAN, declared financial/assessment year and quarter,
independent batch/challan/party counts, dates, tax components, gross challan
arithmetic, party allocation totals and complete crosswalk coverage. Mandatory
field flags have period-dependent exceptions; the importer does not claim all
FVU conditional, legal-code, rate, certificate or CSI checks. No filing receipt,
portal-currentness, bank payment or beneficiary credit is inferred.

The source form requires explicit independent component and gross-deposit
controls, selected current/superseded/cancelled status and a separate clean
retained transaction/section crosswalk. No missing amount becomes zero. The
main INR control is total party tax, not gross challan deposits. The original
published samples illustrate why: 26Q shows 1,900 total party tax against
100,000 gross challan deposit; 27EQ shows 1,000 against 10,000. These original
examples are not client files, independent client expectations or professional
qualification. The numbered tables and samples have FH 18, BH 72, CD 41 and
DD 54 fields; the general-notes FH-16 example is not used to guess the layout.

A crosswalk group names transaction_reference, transaction_date, period,
rule_reference and party_records. Keys are batch/challan/party, for example
1/1/1. Every original party must appear once, with no overlap or omission.
A group can combine multiple parties/challans for one PAN, source section code
and reporting month. Mapped dates and section references are independently
assessed facts retained in the supporting document, not inferred legal mappings.
Expected source rows count groups; the separate party count controls original
DD records. The original tax/payment dates, code, recorded rate, reason flag,
certificate reference and source line stay linked. Gross challans have their
own one-row-per-CD review and export schedule, preserving leading-zero challan
and BSR/Form24G references. Gross amounts are never repeated as party payments.

The output can supply only reported observations in the liability comparison.
It cannot establish expected tax, withholding, payment or credit roles. Current
means selected by this assessment, not independently verified portal status.
Historical source statuses preserve amounts rather than silently becoming zero.
Source rows show the selected history status in the compact default view.

A separate crosswalk is checked for engagement/tenant access, clean scanning,
current retained version and original hash/size. The native file cannot be its
own crosswalk, even if uploaded under another record with identical bytes.
Replacing that document makes both the source and downstream comparison stale
without rewriting their saved bodies. Independently specified expected schedules
are still required. A reviewed prepared statement does not qualify its law or
prove government filing or bank settlement.
