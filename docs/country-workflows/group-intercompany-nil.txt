Explicit nil intercompany population
===================================

The existing IFRS consolidation and intercompany workbenches accept an optional
intercompany_nil_assessment in the approved consolidation assumptions. It is a
shared accounting workflow available to UAE and India engagements. It does not
determine UAE Corporate Tax Group membership or implement its special-purpose
aggregation framework.

Use the existing assumptions editor to record:
  member_engagement_ids: every declared consolidation member exactly once
  period_start / period_end: the exact reporting period of all member packages
  rationale: a meaningful assessment of the complete reciprocal population
  source_record_ids: distinct approved records covering every member engagement

The preparer and reviewer still need authorized access to the member packages.
The calculation checks that all evidence records are approved, belong to the
same tenant and declared member engagements, and cover every member. Their
versions become dependencies of the immutable calculation. Existing freshness
checks continue to control use of a result after a dependency changes.

A nil assessment cannot accompany declared reciprocal balances, elimination
entries or an unrealised-profit elimination. Even a declared zero-value pair
is a population, not an empty population. Missing assessment remains
INTERCOMPANY_POPULATION_UNCONFIRMED; missing transactions never imply nil.

The output retains the complete assessment in intercompany_population with
status EXPLICIT_RETAINED_NIL_ASSESSMENT. This resolves only the missing-population
issue. Group validation remains draft: comparative translation, group equity
rollforward, acquisition work, disclosures, cash eliminations and professional
review retain their separate requirements. Approved records are retained
assessments; the software does not authenticate an absence of transactions.

Verification includes the real PostgreSQL reporting workflow: both member
packages and separate evidence records are approved, the nil result retains
their dependencies, incomplete member evidence and mismatched periods fail,
and existing translation, NCI and non-nil eliminations remain unchanged.
