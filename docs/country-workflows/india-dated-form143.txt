Dated India Form 143 observations

The india-form143-regular-20260722-v1.1 profile reuses original evidence storage,
clean scanning, queued source preparation, independent review, the reported-only
withholding liability comparison, complete party/challan XLSX and source
replacement staleness. It is available only for India, separately from legacy
27EQ. Its explicit tax-year controls currently cover TY 2026-27. It does not
automatically adopt future years, correction statements or another workbook.

This is the retained numbered workbook 1.1 dated in its 22 July 2026 download
URL. The live Protean page labels the link 1.2. Both the workbook and original
TXT sample were fetched again on 30 October 2026 and compared byte-for-byte
with the retained originals. Their hashes, URLs and source conflicts are in
authority-references/form143-2026-07-22/form143-source-assessment.json. No
workbook version was silently relabelled as the current page version.

The numbered layout has FH 18, BH 72, CD 30 and DD 45 fields. Its changed
challan components and DD party/challan reference order cannot use the legacy
27EQ positions. The general-notes example still says FH 16; numbered rows
and actual sample establish the selected FH 18 layout. The compiled limits
retain the exact source workbook SHA-256 and their own byte hash.

The unchanged public sample does not pass this bounded profile. Its CD interest
and other allocations and DD deposited flag are blank despite being marked
mandatory in the numbered table. It also lacks the DDO serial reference that
this non-nil profile explicitly requires for a supported identified challan.
The latter is a conservative profile requirement; it is not a claim that every
issuer-accepted file must satisfy a complete FVU rule implemented here.
Unknown fields are never converted to zero, Y or N. A failed source job has
no result to approve. The public original is preserved unchanged. Separately
labelled synthetic fixtures fill these four fields explicitly for software
verification; those changes are not issuer corrections or client evidence.

Independent counts, reported tax, party allocated deposits, receipt base,
gross challan, tax/interest/fee/other components and interest/other allocations
are all required. The example's 700 party tax, 21,000 receipt base, 210,000
challan tax and 213,000 gross deposit remain different amounts. The profile
does not invent a surcharge/cess split where the new layout does not provide
one. A recorded rate is retained, not used to calculate a legal liability.
The recorded B/C payment mode remains a source claim, not a bank transaction.

Separate party links retain nonresident, PE, section-202 opt-out and deposited
flags, collectee/collection codes, recorded rate, reason/certificate and any
TDS-related reference/date. The deposited flag does not create an executed
payment or posted tax credit. Missing-PAN parties need separate crosswalk
groups. Groups cannot merge different PAN/code/month or recorded tax contexts.
Counts, parents, amounts, fiscal-quarter dates and all four quarters including
January-March rollover are tested without using upload date as the tax year.

The controls and UI deliberately distinguish this tax-year layout from legacy
financial-year inputs. Legacy tax component fields are omitted from its payload,
review and export. Legacy profiles do not receive the new controls or change
their output columns. Required source versions are checked for tenant/engagement
access, clean scanning, original bytes and separate identity/hash; a file cannot
act as its own crosswalk under another evidence record.

API tests use real PostgreSQL/HTTP with explicit scan and queue doubles. They
cover source failure without result, cloned/self/quarantined crosswalk rejection,
self-review rejection, independent review, a separately specified expected
schedule, complete XLSX and unchanged historical bodies after replacement.
Frontend tests exercise the complete source form, version-conflict note, distinct
controls and source claims. Exact final counts and staged source hashes are in
the validation receipt. The hosted native browser case also includes this
separately labelled synthetic completion with actual scanning, form entry,
independent review, export and stale-source capture; execution on the committed
source remains pending until the hosted evidence is inspected.

Complete current issuer-layout/FVU/CSI qualification, all conditional legal-code
rules, certificate eligibility, residence/PE/elections, rates, government filing
and actual client pilots remain open. Form 138/140/144 and corrections require
their own adopted layouts. This observed-file profile does not establish legal
compliance under the 2025 Act. The existing 154 authored synthetic scenarios are
not increased or reclassified as real-client qualification by these tests.
