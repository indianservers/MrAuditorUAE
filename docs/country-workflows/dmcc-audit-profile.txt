DMCC source-pinned authority procedure assessment

This optional GRC obligation profile reuses original evidence, independent
review, exact country/period scope, deadline calculation, filing readiness and
source staleness. It is separate from the existing UAE corporate-tax audit
threshold. A foreign UAE obligation of an India entity remains a foreign
obligation; it does not change the entity country or activate a domestic UAE
tax engine.
Within this profile a registered DMCC company has its own UAE legal-entity
scope; a foreign parent uses a separately declared branch scope. Definitions
on printed page 6 and company status on page 12 of the original amended
regulations were inspected for this distinction; page 12 was also rendered
and visually inspected in this increment.

Select dmcc_audit_scope and its exact profile version in an obligation whose
module is license or governance. Retain the UAE obligation country, explicit
registration reference and financial-year dates. The original amended DMCC
Company Regulations PDF and April 2025 submission-guideline PDF must be separate
current independently approved clean evidence records. The backend checks their
exact pinned SHA-256 values, media type, physical size and actual stored bytes
before validating/approving the record. Derived summaries cannot substitute.
The existing original copies are under authority-references/dmcc-audit-2026-10-30/.

The profile takes explicit assessed membership, legal form, whole-year dormancy,
holding-company status, separate accounts/audit shareholder-notice assessments,
branch group-auditor facts, auditor identity/status, other authority variations,
an adopted operative window and assessment date. No missing boolean defaults
to a legal exemption. Publication/update dates are not inferred commencement
dates. Notice validity, membership, auditor status, period validity and document
substance are professionally assessed inputs; the engine does not authenticate
registries or automatically validate notice ownership/timing thresholds.

Accounts timetable and portal submission use distinct named procedures with an
explicit six-calendar-month period-end rule and Dubai timezone. Registrar
filing uses the retained actual general-meeting event and five business days,
with separate calendar evidence, Dubai weekend exclusions and explicit
trigger-day treatment. A 180-day substitution, different trigger, other working
week or mixed rolling convention is rejected. Extensions require separately
retained original authority evidence through the existing extension controls.
Calendar-month addition preserves the trigger day, clamped to the destination
month's final day; it does not automatically preserve month-end status. For
example, June 30 plus six months produces December 30. The retained original
sources do not resolve every month-counting interpretation. Reviewers must
independently confirm the operative due date for their actual year end; the
December 31 browser fixture does not qualify other month-end interpretations.

Determinations keep individual accounts, audit, group accounts, selected
procedure and auditor requirement separate. A branch group-auditor exception
does not become a portal-submission exemption. Assessed dormant-company notices
are separate; an accounts notice does not automatically become an audit notice.
Unresolved dormancy/notice combinations, dormant group accounts, dormant portal
treatment, other legal forms and authority variations remain visible issues.
Unknown applicability must remain potential. Unresolved issues block independent
approval/locking and filing approval rather than becoming an exemption.

The read-only assessment shows its status, assessment date, source freshness,
separate determinations, issues and limits to authorized GRC readers. Reopening
or replacing a supporting rule/guideline invalidates current filing readiness
without rewriting the saved body. Changing/removing an approved scope or its
guideline reference requires a return to preparing and independent reassessment.
All original filing/approval evidence remains retained. No signing, audit opinion
or external submission is produced. Full appointment consent/eligibility,
auditor-report contents, live approved-auditor registration, complete authority
directions and professional/legal qualification remain separate work.
