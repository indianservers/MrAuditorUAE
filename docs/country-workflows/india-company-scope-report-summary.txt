India company applicability summaries

The report designer offers india-company-scope:standalone/1 and
india-company-scope:consolidated/1 under the India statutory category. These
presets summarize the reviewed company-scope workpaper. They are not complete
statutory audit reports, full CARO clause reports or opinions.

Every preset retains six decisions: small-company status, section 139 company
audit, CARO applicability, standalone CARO matters, consolidated paragraph
3(xxi), and section 143(3)(i) IFC reporting. All six requirements need source
evidence, including false decisions. Not-applicable or narrative overrides
cannot replace these decisions, and no preset item may be omitted.

Prepare and independently review the India company-scope workpaper first. In
the designer, select the matching standalone/consolidated preset, assess each
requirement, and map the requirements to that same reviewed intelligence result.
The default content type is Regulatory checklist. All mapped blocks must refer
to one current resolved scope workpaper for the exact engagement financial
period. A generic checklist or approved audit record cannot substitute for it.

Before template approval and generation, the server rechecks the source contract,
original-byte binding, review, resolved flags, statement scope and exact period.
The generated draft retains the actual decision values and source receipt.
Replacing linked original evidence recursively makes the saved source, template
and generated summary stale while preserving their historical bodies. Independent
preparer restrictions and the existing controlled report export workflow apply.

Preset source metadata names the application's operational contract, not a
purported statutory text. Complete legal-source qualification, applicable audit
clauses, measurement/disclosure completeness and authorized issuance remain
separate requirements. Synthetic test outcomes are not real-client validation.
