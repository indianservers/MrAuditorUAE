Assessed ICFR duty and declared-plan coverage (UAE and India)

Workflow: icfr-duty-coverage
Contract: assessed-icfr-duty-coverage/1
Sources: icfr-duty-scopes and checks, both independently reviewed.

The original controls case requires separate management 404(a) and auditor
404(b) applicability. Generic check labels alone did not preserve that distinction.
This workflow requires an explicit decision for each duty and each declared
subject, with a consistent reporting issuer and issuer/component relationship.
An exemption for one duty never clears the other. Entity country does not select
US reporting obligations. The engagement_country column is the review context,
not the issuer/component domicile or a US legal applicability test. Local India
IFC and UAE duties remain separate.

Applicability is an independently assessed input, not a legal calculation.
Each scope row binds an original authority file and a distinct company/group
assessment by evidence-version UUID and exact SHA256. Import and computation
verify actual bytes, clean status, authorization and latest versions; document
authors are retained as contributors for independent review. Replacement stales
the source and dependent result without rewriting saved values.

Declared adoption windows must cover the complete selected review period.
Pending adoption, unresolved applicability, missing duty decisions and required
duties with no plan remain blocked. Plan identifiers use 404a:<point> or
404b:<point>. Evidence for one namespace does not clear the other. Check expiry,
evidence references and meaningful assessments are checked. Duplicate scope or
test observations, different issuers between duties, wrong country/period and
unplanned evidence cannot silently combine or disappear.

Failed control observations remain exceptions. Review can document a failure;
it never changes that failure into a successful test or an effectiveness opinion.
All outputs retain NOT_DETERMINED effectiveness/professional conclusions and
statutory_calculation_qualified=false. The plan covers only declared subjects and
points; complete design/operating-effectiveness methods, sampling, actual
transactions, filer classification, exemptions, professional attestation and
filing are not established by supported labels or clean document hashes.
The check CSV itself is retained and hash-verified. Its evidence_reference values
remain declared pointers, not authenticated external responses or proof that a
control was performed.

The software does not encode current EGC/public-float/revenue classifications or
infer legal status from location. The independently adopted authority and period
must be assessed from current requirements, including transitions/amendments.
Reference pages below explain the distinction, not a complete adopted rule pack:
https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-corporation-finance/financial-reporting-manual/frm-topic-4
https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-corporation-finance/financial-reporting-manual/frm-topic-10
https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201
These were read on 31 October 2026. The SEC manual includes dated sections and
must not be used to hard-code stale numeric thresholds. The PCAOB page separately
identifies amendments effective 15 December 2026; a future version is not adopted
by this software automatically.
