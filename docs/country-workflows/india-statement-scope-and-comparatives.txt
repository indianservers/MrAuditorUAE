India source statement draft controls

The existing Schedule III source-reconciliation workflow now requires one
consistent declared division across the independently reviewed disclosure
checklist. For Ind AS it also requires explicit, consistent boolean NBFC facts:
Division II for assessed non-NBFCs and Division III for assessed NBFCs. A matching
division on one row cannot hide a contradictory division or NBFC fact elsewhere.
AS remains paired with Division I. This consistency check does not establish
framework eligibility or replace the independently adopted statutory source chain.

Current and comparative trial balances must each balance. Their presented
statement equations must independently reconcile. Every source account needs
an approved group and recognized consistent classification. Comparative-only
accounts remain in the population and require mapping; their zero current
presentation is explicitly marked as absence from the current source. Missing
comparative accounts retain null comparative values rather than fabricated
prior zero balances. Unassessed restatements and FX conversions are not performed.

Rows now retain separate source_snapshot_id and comparative_source_snapshot_id
plus explicit current/comparative account membership. The UI exposes these
references and absence states, together with retained comparative totals and
profit. It never attributes a prior-only account to the current snapshot.

The india-source-statements/2 contract and normalized implementation fingerprint
participate in freshness. Older drafts are retained but stale, including for
downstream report composition; they need regeneration under the new controls.

These are source-reconciled statement drafts. Cash flow, changes in equity,
complete notes, AS/Ind AS measurement, all prescribed disclosures, special
regimes, audit opinions and authorized issuance remain separate requirements.
