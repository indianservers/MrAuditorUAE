ADGM company accounts timetable

The obligation workpaper now accepts an explicit ADGM company timetable scope. It retains the separately assessed accounting reference period, company status immediately before reference end, incorporation/first-anniversary facts, first/subsequent period classification, shortening notices, filing duty and operative version window. The accounts period remains separate; a mapping outside the adopted seven-day bounds requires reassessment. Nothing is selected merely by choosing UAE.

For assessed ordinary periods, the profile requires exactly nine private-company or six public-company months after the reference date. A long first period requires both anniversary and reference-end alternatives. Shortening adds its separate notice alternative, including when the underlying period is a long first period. Month-end preservation is explicit. The latest unadjusted candidate is followed by one following-day adjustment inside a complete, separately evidenced calendar with Saturday/Sunday exclusions. Missing calendar coverage fails validation. Synthetic zero-holiday fixtures are not UAE holiday calendars.

The primary regulations refer to the date of a shortening notice, while the retained guidance describes receipt by the Registrar. Different dates are retained but require separate assessment before approval; this profile does not silently choose the later date. First-period dates/length, unknown company status, unsupported forms, unassessed filing duty, authority modifications and leap-day incorporation anniversaries also remain pending. An assessed exemption has no applicable deadline. This is a timetable over assessed facts, not an automatic exemption test or complete accounts/audit reporting pack.

Extensions retain an aware application timestamp, assessed against the end of the base deadline day in Dubai, plus separate original authority outcome evidence. The extended deadline cannot exceed the adopted three-month or reference-end twelve-month limits. A request is not a receipt or automatically generated accepted filing. This workflow performs no external submission.

The exact retained original regulations and guidance PDFs must be separately uploaded, scanned and independently approved. Both metadata digests and stored object bytes are checked. The original calendar and company facts remain separate sources. The same original-source verifier is reused by DMCC, preserving its independent profile and tests. Approved ADGM scope, source or profile removal requires returning the filing to preparing. Source withdrawal retains the historical body, marks it stale and blocks submission.

The current-version compliance calendar supplies the assessment to the review screen. The panel shows filing duty, reference period, status, classification, issues and source freshness. Missing/mismatched versions do not display a previous determination as current. Damaged saved authority rules project reassessment rather than crashing date/timezone handling. Existing obligations omit the optional new fields.

Synthetic long-first-period input is saved in sample-inputs/SYNTHETIC-adgm-accounts/long-first-period.json. Its UUIDs are placeholders, its operative window and company facts are fictional, and its explicitly zero-holiday calendar is solely an arithmetic fixture. Replace every evidence reference with reviewed original engagement records. It does not count as a qualified client pilot.
