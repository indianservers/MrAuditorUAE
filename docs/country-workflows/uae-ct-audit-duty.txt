UAE corporate-tax audited financial statement duty
==================================================

The country workflow screen offers a dated MD84 of 2025 duty assessment for
one completed, explicitly assessed tax period within the engagement period.
The endpoint is uae-ct-audit-duty under the scoped intelligence engagement
API. Contract: uae-ct-audited-statements-md84-2025/1.

This workflow reuses the evidence store, immutable country-workflow results,
source freshness, separate-reviewer restrictions and full XLSX exports. It
checks preparation/maintenance duty under the adopted decision. It does not
issue an audit opinion, perform an audit, authenticate eligibility or file.

Adopted original
----------------
Retain the exact original MD84 PDF from the Ministry of Finance, SHA-256
1121cdca7e0386dcf93a68a3919ba3f5c42e2521cdaf3c7020c41670dd0afedb.
The unchanged original and source-assessment metadata are in
authority-references/uae-ct-audit-2025. Both complete original pages were
visually inspected. A recreated summary or altered copy is rejected.

Tax periods must commence from 1 January 2025. Earlier periods require their
separate MD82 source rather than automatic application of this profile.
Assessment is after the decision's issuance and after the declared period
end. Retain an independently adopted operative window covering the period
and assessment. Unresolved operative changes keep the current duty unresolved.
These date checks do not establish legal tax-period validity or source adoption.

Assessed facts and separate branches
-----------------------------------
The preparer declares taxable-person, Tax Group and QFZP status explicitly,
with resident/non-resident scope and a complete-period exact AED amount or
explicit unknown. Unknown is never zero. The non-group revenue test uses
strictly more than AED50,000,000; exactly AED50,000,000 does not cross it.
A non-resident's threshold requires the separately assessed UAE PE/nexus
revenue basis; worldwide revenue cannot be substituted. No FX is inferred.

Assessed Tax Groups require audited special-purpose statements independently
of the revenue threshold. Assessed QFZPs have a separate audited-statement
branch. Taxable-person, group and QFZP eligibility are inputs requiring their
own professional evidence; this workflow does not determine them. Conflicting
classifications, material unknowns or unresolved operative changes stay visible.
Designated-zone distribution can require separately prescribed procedures.

Retain a separate clean original company-fact document and its SHA-256. Rule
and fact versions must be distinct, current and byte-verified. Cloning the
original rule cannot establish company facts. All document preparers remain
contributors, so they cannot independently approve their own derived result.

Outputs and boundaries
---------------------
The result screen presents a compact duty summary before the wide source
schedule. It shows the current assessment separately from the dated-source
branch, the exact retained AED revenue (including zero), period and scope,
unresolved issues and separate Tax Group/distribution procedures. Missing
revenue remains not established. It does not round amounts through browser
floating-point numbers or interpret string booleans as assessed answers.

The result keeps the dated-source branch and current assessed requirement,
statement type, threshold/basis, exact amount, unresolved issues and separate
procedure flags. The general entity-audit-exemption flag is always false:
a negative MD84 duty does not waive company-law, free-zone, authority,
contractual or other audit obligations. Full group form, aggregation, audit
and submission procedures require separate sources; this profile invents
no filing deadline. Publication, statutory signoff and government filing are
not executed. Replacement of retained rule or fact evidence makes the
historical result stale without changing its body or original review history.

Testing uses explicitly synthetic entity facts and thresholds with the
unchanged public rule original. These fixtures do not constitute independent
professional or real-client qualification. Browser acceptance is source-pinned
to its own hosted result; local tests do not substitute for browser evidence.
