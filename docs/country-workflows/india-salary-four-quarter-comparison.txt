India salary: four-quarter / annual current-employer comparison
==============================================================

The country workflow screen and source preparation screen offer a separate
four-quarter salary comparison for India. It reuses the native historical
24Q sources, independent review, immutable events, evidence store, freshness
checks and XLSX export. Endpoint: salary-annual-comparison under the scoped
intelligence engagement API. Contract: india-salary-four-quarter-current-employer/1.

The target is SD field 36 of the retained Q4 v7.5 workbook: current-employer
tax deducted for the whole year, described by that source as the aggregate
of Annexure I tax across the four quarters for the employee. It is NOT SD29,
which also includes other employers, superannuation and other TDS/TCS.
The adopted comparison covers FY2013-14 to FY2025-26, with explicit field36
values. A blank field36 remains missing, even if quarterly amounts are zero.
This compares source observations; it does not recompute tax or legal liability.

Inputs
------
Choose one current independently reviewed original regular source for each
non-nil quarter: v6.3 Q1-Q3 and v7.5 Q4. All sources must share the same TAN,
financial year and exact selected quarter. Q4 must carry complete controlled
annual records. The engagement financial period must cover the whole year.
Q1-Q3 can instead carry explicit retained nil-quarter assessments. Missing
selections do not become nil. Q4 cannot be nil in this annual comparison.

Retain a separate clean annual employee/nil-quarter crosswalk document and
its original SHA-256. Original native-file bytes, quarterly crosswalk versions
and byte-identical clones cannot substitute for this document. The original
document must be current, hash-verified and within the existing bounded
evidence verification path. Its preparer and all source preparers remain
contributors for independent-review restrictions.

Map every annual SD/batch/record key exactly once. Each assigned party link
identifies its quarter and original batch/challan/party key. One quarterly
party cannot be assigned twice. Different valid PANs require corrected
evidence. Names and missing-PAN sentinels never automatically identify an
employee: the mapping retains an explicit identity status and assessment.
Unresolved identities stay unresolved even where amounts agree.

Outputs and exceptions
----------------------
Each salary row retains the original annual source line, four separate
quarterly totals, their sum, recorded current-employer tax, signed difference,
identity assessment and comparison status. Unassigned quarterly parties
remain explicit exception rows and in their own complete lineage population.
They prevent a complete-coverage claim and require documented disposition
before workpaper approval. Specified-senior annual records are disclosed as
outside this salary comparison; 94P quarterly parties cannot be assigned to SD.

The party coverage view pages assigned and unassigned populations separately.
XLSX contains complete Salary quarter links and Unassigned salary parties
sheets, in addition to the result schedule. Quarterly source events, context
and independent annual mapping are retained dependencies. Replacement of
either an annual mapping or a quarterly mapping makes the historical result
stale, preserving its original body and review history.

Limits
------
Four selected prepared statements or an assessed nil quarter do not establish
government filing completeness, portal-currentness, FVU/CSI validation,
bank payment, tax credit or professional signoff. No new salary tax rates,
deduction entitlement, pension comparison or legal certificate conclusions
are inferred. Real-client qualification still requires supplied redacted
inputs, independent expected schedules and retained professional reviews.
