Dated India Form 140 and Form 144 imports
=======================================

Implemented profiles: india-form140-regular-20260722-v1.1 and
india-form144-regular-20260722-v1.1. These are prepared-statement
observations for India, TY 2026-27, Q1-Q4, in INR. They reuse the numbered
withholding reader and the existing independent evidence/review/export flow.

Original sources
---------------
Publisher page: https://tinpan.proteantech.in/downloads/e-tds/eTDS-download-regular.html
The page identifies v1.2 while these downloaded workbooks identify v1.1.
Exact original workbooks and public samples are retained alongside the code:
authority-references/form140-2026-07-22 and form144-2026-07-22.
The compiled layouts pin the original workbook SHA-256, not the page label.
Both layouts contain FH18, BH72, CD30 and DD45 fields.

The unchanged public samples omit mandatory challan/allocated amounts and
the deposited flag. The Form 144 sample also omits its reason-A certificate.
They are rejected. Unit/API tests complete separate explicitly synthetic
copies; originals are never repaired or represented as qualified client data.

Controls and retained observations
----------------------------------
Each import requires its own independently retained crosswalk, complete party
population, batch/challan/party counts, tax deducted, allocated deposits,
gross challan deposits, payment bases, challan tax/interest/fee/penalty,
allocated interest/penalty, and both explicit cash-withdrawal totals.
Positive deducted tax can remain undeposited: the N flag requires zero
allocation, rather than fabricating payment. General/cooperative cash fields
are conditional on the source section codes and independently controlled.

Form 144 preserves recorded nonresident status, regime opt-out, destination,
remittance, gross-up, rate basis, residence/contact/tax-ID fields and references.
Its missing-PAN low-rate reason-C source condition requires all four residence
fields. Unknown filler fields remain blank-only. These recorded fields do not
establish treaty entitlement, legal rates, certificate authenticity or residency.

The Form 144 reference label mentions Form15CA while its remark mentions
Form121. The reader retains the generic reference without claiming either
filing or acknowledgment authenticity. Complete annexure/code qualification,
FVU/CSI, tax-credit matching and portal submission remain outside this profile.

Shared date correction
----------------------
For dated Forms 140/143/144, the original DD27 remarks set the beginning of
the payment quarter as the earliest deduction/collection date. They do not
set its end as the latest date. The shared reader now retains late tax dates
after that quarter, up to the independently supplied observation date.
Payment dates remain bounded by the declared payment quarter. Dates requiring
a tax-year mapping after 31 March 2027 are explicitly rejected pending a
separate assessed mapping. Ordinary Form 143 outputs retain exact compatibility
in three complete-payload regression comparisons (base, zero-tax and Q4).

Acceptance limits
-----------------
Synthetic parser, HTTP review/export/staleness and frontend tests exercise
the implementation. They do not constitute professional or production signoff.
The latest changes have not yet received a dedicated hosted browser run.
Real redacted pilot inputs and independent expected results belong in
pilot-data/India and pilot-data/UAE. These folders contain no real client
qualification evidence until such files and approvals are supplied.
