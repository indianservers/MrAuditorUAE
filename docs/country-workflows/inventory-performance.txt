Committed stock and gross-profit comparison — common UAE/India/global arithmetic

Purpose: the stock-audit reference includes current/prior closing-stock movement
and gross-profit trends, distinct from physical verification and bank drawing
power. Source reviewed 2026-10-30: https://coraa.ai/solutions/stock-audit . Its FAQ
expressly excludes drawing-power calculation. This feature addresses its actual
ledger-side analytical requirement; it does not implement lending calculations.

Contract: committed-inventory-performance/1. Entry: Run workflows, then "Review
stock and gross-profit trends from committed accounts". POST endpoint:
/api/v1/intelligence/engagements/{eid}/inventory-performance . Options and account
selectors expose authorized active committed sources for the same entity. Current
snapshot must match the engagement period. Prior snapshot must precede it.

Select inventory, revenue and cost-of-sales accounts separately for both periods.
Each group requires at least one actual retained account (maximum 100), an
independently entered count and signed amount, and a scope assessment. Selections
within a period cannot overlap. No account name, country code or chart prefix
automatically determines a metric. Missing selected accounts or control differences
are rejected. A zero inventory total requires its own nil/netting assessment and
actual ledger-account selection; a missing account is never silently zero.

Measurement bases:
Inventory: debit-normal closing balance.
Revenue: period credits less debits, excluding opening balances.
Cost of sales: period debits less credits, excluding opening balances.
Gross profit: revenue less cost of sales.
Gross-profit margin: gross profit divided by positive net revenue, times 100.
Stock change percentage: current minus prior stock, divided by positive prior
stock, times 100. Gross-profit margin change is in percentage points, not percent.
Reversals retain their sign. Nonpositive denominators leave percentages null and
surface a review exception; no zero percentage is invented.

Users explicitly assess period/accounting-policy comparability, display rounding
and thresholds. Threshold comparison uses unrounded arithmetic and strict greater
than, before display rounding. There are no UAE/India rate, margin or materiality
defaults. An assessed-incomparable period or an unbalanced source blocks approval.
Other analytical exceptions require documented dispositions before independent
approval. Account/source preparers cannot approve their own output.

The arithmetic observation states the current/prior margin and assessed-threshold
signal; it does not infer causes or an audit conclusion. Different period lengths
remain visible and are not silently annualized. Both snapshots use the entity's
explicit functional currency; no FX conversion or currency relabelling occurs.

All account links retain snapshot ID, account ID/code/name, opening, debit, credit,
closing and normalized amount. Six independent control records remain separate.
The UI pages account lineage independently of the five metric rows; all links and
controls are retained in dedicated XLSX sheets. Raw arithmetic remains in saved
rows and the full-column view. Source supersession stales the saved result without
rewriting its body or version.

This is source-account selection arithmetic, not proof of complete classification,
source-system completeness, physical attendance, inventory condition/title/NRV,
tax treatment, framework applicability or a statutory audit opinion. Imported TB
debit/credit semantics still require the existing source mapping and independent
controls; TB arithmetic is not transaction-level audit evidence. UAE/India legal
and professional reporting packs remain separate. Real-client pilot qualification
and the full original roadmap remain open. Authored fixtures are synthetic only.
