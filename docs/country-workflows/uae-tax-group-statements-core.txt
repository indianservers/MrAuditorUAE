UAE Tax Group four-statement workpaper core
=========================================

Status: internal calculation layer; not yet an available client workflow.
Contract: uae-tax-group-four-statement-workpaper/1.
Implementation: backend/app/modules/ifrs/uae_tax_group_statements.py.

This implements part of the distinct FTA7/CTP007 aggregation framework. It
does not repurpose IFRS10 consolidation or claim a completed reporting pack.
No endpoint, source-byte verification, independent review or user interface
is connected to this engine yet. Source binding remains false, statutory
issuance remains false, professional qualification remains NOT_ESTABLISHED
and validation.ready is always false. These boundaries also apply when the
numbers reconcile and no input issue is detected.

Inputs and calculation
----------------------
Every current-period member supplies an explicit whole-period standalone,
unconsolidated AED package with legal-entity identity, parent/subsidiary role,
membership coverage, financial year, accounting standard, policy assessment,
source-version identity/hash and independently mapped statement rows.
Exactly one parent, unique entities, aligned financial years and all four
statements are required. There is no automatic FX or mid-period alignment.

The statements are position, profit/loss, OCI and changes in equity. Position
uses debit-positive/credit-negative balances. Performance uses income-positive
and expense-negative amounts; equity movements use increase-positive values.
Opening/closing equity, profit transfers, OCI and nil categories must be
explicit, rather than silently supplied from missing fields.

Decimal strings retain up to eight decimal places without rounding. Binary
floats, booleans, unbounded strings and exponent notation are rejected. The
48-digit calculation context accommodates sums beyond an individual input's
28-digit bound. A complete period is limited to20000 statement rows across
at most100 members, with separately bounded explicit adjustments/exceptions.

Each output caption retains all member source references, labels, original
amounts, adjustments, final amounts, treatment and source-version hashes.
Adjustments target existing source rows and require nonzero explicit amounts,
source references and reasons. No elimination or journal is invented.

Framework distinctions
----------------------
Member investments, member equity and direct member-investment impairments
are protected from intramember elimination. Nonmember investments have a
separate cost-less-impairment bridge; missing or inconsistent cost evidence
stays unresolved. A nonmember cannot be an elimination counterparty.

Other intramember balances/transactions need an exact actual member pair and
transaction reference. Uneliminated amounts remain issues. Consequential
asset adjustments also need that exact transaction binding. Uniform-policy,
UAE CT and consolidation-only acquisition adjustments remain separate.

UAE current/deferred CT amounts and consolidation-only acquisition entries
remaining in the output are issues. Foreign/emirate taxes cannot be removed
as UAE CT. The separate-legal-entity consolidation exclusion cannot remove
standalone business-acquisition assets/goodwill. Pre-UAE-CT profit is null
while identified UAE CT performance amounts remain unresolved; the original
profit/loss sum is retained separately. Equity effects of any adjustment
must be explicitly supported and reconciled, not silently balanced.

Pre-join loss exceptions bind exact member transactions and retained declared
loss/reversal facts. An unreversed deductible loss prevents premature
elimination. The separate income-ceiling schedule caps declared period income
at the remaining declared loss. This is a review schedule, not a determination
of taxable income. Every such exception stays flagged for its accounting/tax
bridge and independent professional assessment.

Each original member package and the adjusted group have separate position,
equity rollforward, equity-to-position, profit-transfer and OCI-transfer
checks. Equal/opposite source errors cannot disappear into a balanced group.
Numerical reconciliation does not clear unassessed populations or policies.

Comparatives and notes
----------------------
Only the actual first tax period of the Tax Group has no group comparative.
An existing group in its first2025 framework year still requires the preceding
tax period. Comparative packages have their own member population, source
lineage, statement rows, adjustments, notes and checks. Prior-only captions
are retained in the prior statements; prior values are not inferred from
current values. An unassessed current-framework comparative basis stays an
issue. Changed membership is retained explicitly, not rewritten to match the
current population; professional treatment is not established by this core.

Basis of preparation, policies, estimates/judgments and membership disclosures
must be supplied separately for each period. Meaningful text is required,
but the exact prescribed wording and completeness are not authenticated here.
