UAE company-law accounts and written-request duties
===================================================

The UAE country-workflow screen offers an accounts-duty assessment for one
completed fiscal year and either one declared written request, an explicitly
assessed absence of requests, or an unresolved request population. It reuses
the existing document store, immutable country-workflow results, separate
reviewers, source freshness and exports. Endpoint: POST intelligence/
engagements/{eid}/uae-company-accounts-duty.
Contract: uae-ccl-accounts-articles26-27/1.

Required originals and independent adoption
-------------------------------------------
Retain four distinct current, clean evidence versions with distinct hashes:
  1. Exact Ministry-hosted English FDL32/2021 PDF, SHA-256
     b08a904a9af7102804e96e70c77a975876f3f2705190ecb6bea4f39d533435af.
  2. Exact linked Arabic FDL20/2025 amendment PDF, SHA-256
     7e035b6c3616255ffe8f1c4326080bec3f270f165aa63eeb06ab4c1f481e7163.
  3. Separate engagement-specific operative-source adoption evidence, with SHA.
  4. Separate company/fiscal-year/request facts, with SHA.

The English PDF is explicitly marked unofficial translation. Its publication
by the Ministry does not make it a current consolidated or legally adopted
English version. The amendment changes applicability provisions including
Articles 3 and 5. The preparer records the independently checked source chain,
operative window, publication date, later changes and whole-period company
applicability/exclusions. The software does not authenticate those assessments.
No actual Gazette date is hardcoded or inferred from issue date or download
metadata. A declared publication date produces a next-day commencement
observation under the retained amendment wording. Unresolved publication,
source adoption or other changes keep current duties unresolved while retaining
the dated-source branch. This is not automatic global legal adoption.

All originals are verified against stored bytes within the existing 100MiB
verification bound. Cloned roles, wrong originals, wrong hashes, quarantined
or superseded versions fail. Unavailable/corrupt original files fail without
exposing private object paths. Every original-file preparer remains a
contributor and cannot independently approve the derived assessment.

Assessed company-law branches
----------------------------
The fiscal year must match the engagement exactly, be complete by assessment,
and commence from 2 January 2022. The retained source window covers the entire
year and assessment. Fiscal-year validity under Article 28 remains a separate
independently assessed fact; the software does not infer registration dates,
first-year 6–18-month validity or subsequent-year 12-month validity from a GL.

Within the explicitly assessed law scope, the profile retains Article 26
accounting-register duty and its minimum five-year retention observation,
and Article 27 annual accounts and international accounting-basis duties.
Assessed LLCs and public/private joint-stock companies have the separate
annual-audit branch. Partnership branches do not establish a general audit
exemption. An unknown legal form cannot become a negative annual-audit result.
Revenue does not select this company-law branch; the separate MD84 corporate-
tax assessment can be negative while an assessed LLC's company-law audit
duty remains positive. Neither result establishes a company-law/free-zone
exemption. No destruction date, complete retention policy, actual financial
statements, audit opinion or filing is generated by this duty assessment.

One written request
-------------------
Record request date and assessed partner/shareholder eligibility separately.
Confirm whether the selected year is the last audited accounts requested,
holding-company status, actual response status/date and each delivered copy.
The accounts and auditor-report copies remain separate; a holding company
also needs the group accounts copy. Unknown holding status is not non-holding.
A timely response date alone does not establish complete delivery.

A ten-day candidate date is calculated only after an independently retained
calendar-day/exclude-receipt counting assessment. That entire response period
must lie within the adopted source window. Unassessed counting produces no
deadline. Unassessed response status does not become an assumed non-response.
An explicitly assessed lack of response distinguishes not-yet-due, due-on-
assessment-date and overdue. A known response after the candidate date retains
its late observation even when all copies were delivered. Missing/unknown
copies, inconsistent response facts and unclear last-audited-year scope remain
visible issues. These observations do not authenticate legal counting,
requester identity, actual delivery or professional conclusions.

Validation and subsequent changes
---------------------------------
Local checks include every legal-form branch, uncertain source/adoption facts,
request boundaries, null/false distinctions, source-role cloning, exact-period
and country controls, byte verification, separate review and full XLSX fields.
Replacing any of the four document roles stales the original result without
rewriting it. A changed legal-adoption document does not stale the independent
MD84 result; changed shared company facts stale both dependent assessments.
The synthetic publication date used in fixtures is not an assertion of the
actual Gazette date. Synthetic checks do not establish real-client or
professional qualification. Hosted browser/scanner evidence remains a separate
source-specific gate.
